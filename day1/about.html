About QuickNotes

QuickNotes helps you capture ideas and reminders quickly. Keep useful thoughts in one simple place so you can come back to them later.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your idea or reminder in the new note field.
  2. Select Add Note to save it to your list.
  3. Review your notes whenever you need a reminder.

Features

  • Quickly capture ideas and reminders.
  • View your notes together in one list.
  • See how many notes you have.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the note in the new note field.
Tab Move to the next form control or link.
